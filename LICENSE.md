## Obstacle

Wooden crate by Shadowman.
https://opengameart.org/content/crates-2
Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal.
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The file in the project is
`Content/Materials/Textures/T_Wood.uasset`
(crate2_5). One quarter of that texture is
packed into `T_Atlas_Packed.png`, palette row 5,
and that is what one cube face shows. Credit
is not required. The bump map from the pack
is not used.
